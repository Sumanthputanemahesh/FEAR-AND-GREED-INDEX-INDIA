    Fear & Greed Indicators

    Data — Yahoo Finance (Nifty 50 and its constituents, India VIX, LTGILTBEES gilt ETF); NSE F&O UDiFF bhavcopy (Put/Call). Rebuilt automatically each weekday evening IST.
    Method — each signal is scored 0–100 by its rolling 252-day percentile rank, then equally weighted. Volatility and Put/Call are inverted, so a high reading always means greed.
    Core vs Extended — Put/Call only reaches back to mid-2025 because NSE's UDiFF bhavcopy format did not exist before July 2024. Core uses the five long-history signals and is the version to use for analysis; Extended adds Put/Call. They correlate at 0.96 but are not interchangeable.
    Junk bond demand, the seventh CNN component, is omitted: India has no liquid free daily high-yield versus investment-grade spread series.
