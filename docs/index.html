Method & caveats

How the composite is built

  1. Each of the six raw signals is ranked against its own trailing 252 trading days.
  2. That rank becomes a 0–100 score — the percentile of days it beats.
  3. Volatility and put/call are inverted, so a high score always means greed.
  4. The composite is the equal-weighted mean of whichever scores are available that day.
0–25 Extreme Fear 26–45 Fear 46–55 Neutral 56–75 Greed 76–100 Extreme Greed

Sources & limitations

Built entirely from free data — Yahoo Finance for price, volatility (India VIX) and constituent-level breadth; NSE's own F&O bhavcopy, parsed directly, for a native Nifty options put/call ratio; a gilt ETF standing in for bond returns. A credit-spread component is deliberately omitted — no free, liquid, daily India high-yield-versus-investment-grade series exists. Same construction methodology as the companion UK index — see the repository for exact sources. Not investment advice.
